Privacy Policy

Melody — High-Fidelity Music Player (com.melody.music)
Effective Date: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Melody ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Melody Android application (mobile & Android TV) and web player at melody-chi-nine.vercel.app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Anonymous Device Identifier: A randomly generated app-scoped device ID is stored locally on your device to enable recently played history, playback continuity, and optional multi-device sync without requiring an account.
  • Optional Account / Licence Information: If you choose to register for a free VIP sync licence key inside the app, we collect the name, optional email address, and mobile number you voluntarily submit solely to issue and sync your licence key, playlists, and favourites across your devices.
  • Playback & Library Preferences: Your liked tracks, custom playlists, equalizer settings, and recently played history are saved to provide personalized playback features.
  • Offline Downloaded Audio (Android App Only): Songs you explicitly download for offline listening are stored privately inside the app's sandboxed local storage on your device. Melody never reads your personal files, photos, or contacts.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services

The Melody Android mobile app uses Google AdMob to display a brief app-open/interstitial ad to help keep the service free. Google AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID (AD_ID), approximate coarse network information, and ad interaction metrics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time via your Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization / Delete Advertising ID.

3. Android App Permissions & Why We Need Them

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to stream music over encrypted HTTPS and detect offline/online status cleanly.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required by Android to keep music playing smoothly in the background and when your screen is locked.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to display the system Lock Screen and Notification Shade playback controls (Play/Pause, Previous, Next, Like, Shuffle).
  • WAKE_LOCK: Prevents audio stuttering or mid-song interruptions while streaming with the screen off.

Melody does not request access to your Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, SMS, Call Logs, or External Photo Gallery.

4. Data Security

All network communication between the Melody app and our servers is strictly encrypted using HTTPS/TLS (cleartext HTTP traffic is disabled at the OS level). Authentication tokens are stored in private app preferences excluded from public storage and Android cloud backups.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

You may log out at any time from the Profile screen to immediately erase local session tokens, or uninstall the app to remove all locally cached offline tracks and preferences. To request permanent deletion of any optional licence registration data or cloud playlists stored on our servers, email us with your registered mobile number or licence key, and we will process your deletion request within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

Melody is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app improvements or regulatory requirements. Updated revisions will always be published at this URL with the effective date updated above.
